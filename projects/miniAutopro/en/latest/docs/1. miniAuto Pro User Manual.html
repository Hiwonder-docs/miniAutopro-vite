1.1 Overview of miniAuto Pro ​

1.1.1 Product Introduction ​

miniAuto Pro is an open-source Mecanum-wheel robot car with large AI model capabilities designed for beginners. Developed with an ESP32 controller, it features high-performance hardware, including an all-metal Mecanum chassis, Hall encoder gear motors, an RGB ultrasonic module, and a 4-channel line follower sensor. The robot car supports three programming methods, including Scratch, Python, and Arduino. It offers extensive tutorial cases and open-source underlying code, making it suitable for learning basic motion control and sensor applications.

1.2 Assembly Guide ​

  1. Assemble the ESP32 UNO expansion board, the ESP32 UNO board, and the chassis brackets in sequence. Pay attention to the orientation during installation, and ensure that the DC ports of both boards face the same direction.
  1. Secure the motors beneath the brackets using eight PM3 × 25 screws and M3 nuts.
  1. Connect the four motors to the ESP32 UNO expansion board using motor cables. Refer to the diagram below for the mapping between motor ports and expansion board ports.
  1. Install the four Mecanum wheels onto the output shafts of the motors using four couplers and M2.3 × 25 pan head screws.
  1. Place the 7.4 V battery in the center of the chassis brackets, and route the DC output cable and charging connector through the reserved hole in the brackets to the opposite side. Secure the battery cover using four PM3 × 5 screws, and then plug the battery DC output cable into the DC port of the ESP32 UNO expansion board.
  1. Secure the M4 × 30 double-pass nylon standoffs onto the brackets using two PM4 × 8 screws, and then attach the 4-channel line follower sensor to the other end of the nylon standoffs. Connect the 4-channel line follower sensor to any I2C port on the ESP32 UNO expansion board using a 4-pin module cable.
  1. Secure the ultrasonic module onto the front cover plate, ensuring that the I2C port of the ultrasonic module faces upward during installation.
  1. Connect the I2C port of the ultrasonic module to any I2C port on the ESP32 UNO expansion board using a 4-pin module cable. Fasten the front and rear covers to the chassis brackets in sequence, and then attach the top cover plate onto the front and rear covers.

1.3 Charging and Precautions ​

1.3.1 Battery Charging and Installation ​

Due to shipping regulations, lithium batteries cannot be fully charged prior to delivery. Charge the battery for approximately 1 hour before first use.

The charging procedure is as follows:

  1. Charge the battery using the provided charger. Disconnect the charger only after the charger indicator turns green, indicating a full charge.

NOTE

  • Never reverse the battery polarity.

  • Ensure the charging adapter output is 8.4 V.

  1. Place miniAuto Pro on an open, flat surface, and push the power switch to ON to power on the robot car.

1.3.2 Battery Safety Precautions ​

  1. Use the charger provided in the kit to charge the battery.

  2. The adapter indicator glows red during charging and turns green when the battery is fully charged. Disconnect the power supply in a timely manner after charging to prevent overcharging.

  3. When not using the robot for an extended period, fully charge the battery, toggle the switch on the battery box to OFF, and store the battery in a cool, dry place.

  4. Modification, soldering, or unauthorized alteration of the battery charger or lithium battery is strictly prohibited.

  5. Store the battery away from high temperatures and liquids to prevent overheating, fire, moisture damage, capacity degradation, or functional failure.

Statement: Hiwonder assumes no liability for product damage, financial loss, or safety accidents resulting from improper use non-compliant with this manual.

1.3.3 Device Operating Precautions ​

  1. Connect the device via the Wonderbot app bundled with miniAuto Pro. Pairing via passcode in the mobile phone system settings is prohibited.
  2. Avoid using the ultrasonic module to continuously detect objects at extremely close range.
  3. Low battery levels may prevent the robot from operating properly. Maintain the battery voltage above 7 V.
  4. If line following performance is suboptimal, adjust the sensitivity of the line follower sensor by referring to 4. Extended Learning Materials/6. 4-Channel Line Follower Sensor Specifications.
  5. If the robot car fails to move laterally, refer to 1.2 Assembly Guide to verify whether the Mecanum wheels and wiring are installed correctly.
  6. For the Ultimate Kit, observe the following additional precautions:
  • Do not forcibly twist or turn the servos after power-on to prevent servo damage.
  • Avoid keeping the servos at limit positions for extended periods to prevent servo stall.
  • Keep fingers clear of gripper joints to prevent pinching injuries.
  • Servos are precision components and consumables, requiring replacement after prolonged or excessive operation.
  1. Observe the following precautions during product operation and storage:
  • This product contains small components and sharp pins, making it unsuitable for children under 12 years old.
  • Minors must operate this product under adult supervision and guidance.
  • This product contains small and sharp parts. Swallowing or pressing on parts must be avoided to prevent injury.
  • This product contains conductive parts. Contact with metal objects during power-on is prohibited.
  • Do not forcibly bend or force machine parts after power-on to avoid structural damage.
  • When the product remains idle for an extended period, fully charge the battery, remove it from the car, and store it in a cool, dry place.
